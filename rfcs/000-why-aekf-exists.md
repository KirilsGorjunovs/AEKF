
# RFC-000 — Why AEKF Exists

**Status:** Draft
**Version:** 0.1

## Abstract

AEKF proposes a vendor-neutral architecture for software engineering knowledge.
Instead of organising knowledge around AI tools, prompts, or agent-specific skills,
it organises knowledge around engineering decisions.

## Core Principle

> Engineering knowledge must outlive the AI agent consuming it.

## Vision

Codex, Claude, Gemini, Cursor and future coding assistants should consume the
same engineering knowledge through different renderers.

## Problem

Current knowledge is fragmented across prompts, skills, hooks, repository
instructions and vendor-specific mechanisms.

## Scope

AEKF defines how engineering knowledge should be organised, composed,
discovered, measured and rendered.
